Natural & chemical-free — our solution

Most clothing ends up in landfills, adding to soil, water and air pollution. Our project tackles this with circular economy principles — giving discarded denim a second life instead of a final resting place, and using neem, a tree every Indian grandmother trusts, as a natural finish.
