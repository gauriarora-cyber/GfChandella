Circular Economy in Action

Like the eternal chakra, nothing is wasted — every material keeps moving through the cycle.

  • Maximise resource use — every scrap of denim finds a purpose.
  • Minimise waste — leftover pieces become scrunchies.
  • Extend product life — old jeans live on as durable bags.
