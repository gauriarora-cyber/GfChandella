The Neem Finish Process

Five simple steps rooted in traditional Indian knowledge.

👖1

Preparation

Cut, wash and dry old jeans.

🍃2

Neem Extract

Boil fresh neem leaves in water.

🫗3

Soaking

Soak the fabric in extract for 20–30 minutes.

☀️4

Drying

Dry in shade and press lightly.

👜5

Final Product

A neem-finished denim bag is ready!
